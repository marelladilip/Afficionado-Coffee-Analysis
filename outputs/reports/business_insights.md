# Afficionado Coffee Roasters
## Business Insights & Recommendations

### 1. Overall Business Performance

The business generated $698,812.33 in revenue from 149,116 transactions,
with 214,470 units sold.

The average transaction value was $4.69.

### 2. Store Performance

Hell's Kitchen generated the highest revenue at $236,511.17.

However, the three stores showed relatively balanced performance, with
revenue differences remaining small across locations.

### 3. Category Performance

Coffee was the leading product category with $269,952.45 in revenue.

Coffee and Tea together generated approximately 66.7% of total revenue,
indicating strong dependence on the core beverage categories.

### 4. Hourly Demand

10 AM recorded the highest hourly revenue at $88,673.39.

The strongest demand occurred between approximately 8 AM and 10 AM.

### 5. Time-of-Day Performance

Morning generated $388,288.67, representing approximately 55.6% of total
revenue.

Morning demand is therefore the most important time period for the business.

---

## Recommendations

1. Ensure adequate inventory availability during the morning peak.
2. Prioritize operational readiness during the 8 AM–10 AM period.
3. Continue monitoring Coffee and Tea performance because they represent
   the majority of revenue.
4. Compare store-level product mix to identify location-specific
   opportunities.
5. Investigate lower-demand evening periods for potential improvement.
